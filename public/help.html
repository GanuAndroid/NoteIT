Frequently Asked Questions

How do I share my notes? expand_more
You can share notes by clicking the 'Share' icon at the top right of any note. You'll be prompted to enter the recipient's email address or generate a public link.
What happens when I delete a note? expand_more
When you delete a note you own, it is removed permanently from our servers. Action cannot be undone.
